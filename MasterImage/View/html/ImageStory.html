How to use?

  1. Scan the provided QR Code to upload a picture from your phone. NOTE: You are given 30 seconds to accomplish this step.
  2. After uploading the photo, choose a template for your photo.
  3. Choose a language for the story caption.
  4. Choose your preferred story.
  5. Choose to save the image story or not.
  6. Scan the provided QR code to save the image story.
