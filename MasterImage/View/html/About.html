Master Image Photo Center is a photo shop that began its journey on June 1, 2013, when German P. Lumanglas took over what was once known as KODAK under a different owner. Located on the 3rd Floor of the Macli-ing Building in Poblacion, Bontoc, Mountain Province, the shop has grown into a trusted place for photo editing and printing services. Operating from 8:00 AM onwards, it continues to serve the community with dedication and passion.

The name “Master Image” reflects Mr. Lumanglas’ expertise - “Master” for his skill in various editing techniques, and “Image” for his ability to perfect lighting and details to produce high-quality photographs. His commitment to excellent craftsmanship and friendly service has earned the shop a reputation for delivering polished, premium results.

What sets Master Image Photo Center apart is its ongoing effort to innovate and enhance the customer experience. Aside from offering dependable service and quality outputs, the shop now features its own customer entertainment app, designed to keep clients engaged while waiting for their IDs to be processed. The app also includes an AI-powered story generator that creates unique narratives based on each photo, making every visit more enjoyable and memorable.
